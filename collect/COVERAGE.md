## Why the remaining columns are not filled

- **Licence reading (11 columns):** `license_type`, `license_check`, `license_restrictions`, `license_evidence`, `license_spdx_confirmed`, `license_osi_approved`, `license_fsf_libre`, `license_attribution_required`, `license_commercial_use`, `license_derivatives_allowed`, `license_share_alike`. These are judgments from reading each licence text. The scripts take only the licence fields that the registries declare.
- **Discovery and identity (4 columns):** `repo_source`, `npm_package_source`, `release_status`, `in_v1`. These record how each candidate was found and how it stood in the earlier list. The scripts do not reconstruct them.
- **Sources the scripts cannot read (2 columns):** `npm_dependents` (no registry endpoint gives it) and `npm_jsdelivr_hits_30d` (jsDelivr gives only the last 30 days from the day of the run, so the cut-off window cannot be read; the collector leaves the column blank).
